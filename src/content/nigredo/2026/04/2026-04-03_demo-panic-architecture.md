Built something sturdy out of anxiety this week. Load-bearing anxiety — the kind you cannot remove without the whole ceiling coming down on top of you. The walls are woken-up-at-3am. The floor is every message I should have sent two days ago. The roof, somehow, is a person I used to love and a list of errands that have become moral in a way errands were never supposed to be.

I am living in this house. It has excellent foundations. I hate it here.

The people who visit say the light is nice at this angle. They do not notice that there are no doors.
