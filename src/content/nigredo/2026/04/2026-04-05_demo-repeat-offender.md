I have now done the thing I specifically told myself, in writing, at 2am on a Tuesday in January, I would never do again. I remember writing the promise. I remember meaning it. I remember the particular quality of conviction that felt, at the time, like it would be load-bearing across the rest of my life.

It was not load-bearing. It was Tuesday-shaped. It has not survived into April.

I am now, apparently, the kind of person who can look myself in the eye in the mirror and think: we have been here before and we will be here again, and the seeing does not stop the doing.

Maybe the seeing was the thing I was using to feel like I was different. Maybe the seeing was decoration, not braking.
