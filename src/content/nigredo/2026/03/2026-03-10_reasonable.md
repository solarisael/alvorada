I am tired of being reasonable. I am tired of the restraint it requires and the way everyone mistakes it for peace.

It is not peace. It is the specific silence of someone who has decided the argument is not worth what it would cost them. That is different. Wildly different. And I am running out of the currency that buys that silence.
