Morning light reaches him like an {{fx:glow:1.3}}apology{{/fx}}.

Cinza wakes in stone silence. No memory answers when he calls inward, only a pressure behind the ribs and an {{fx:flicker:1.1:0.9}}old fear with no name{{/fx}}. The cave ceiling is low enough to touch. He does not touch it.

{{fx:memory_fragment:1.1:0.85}}

{{fx:whisper:1.2}}"You have been here before. The second time is always quieter."{{/fx}}

A name surfaces and dissolves before he can read it.

{{/fx}}

He sits up slowly. The interface that arrives at the edge of his vision is {{fx:glitch:1.2:1.0}}fragmented, missing half its labels{{/fx}}, insisting on his attention while offering nothing useful.

{{fx:terminal:1.2:0.9}}

[SYSTEM] Identity index: incomplete.

[SYSTEM] {{fx:glitch:1.1:1.2}}Last known checkpoint: corrupted.{{/fx}}

[SYSTEM] Proceeding on minimal state.

{{/fx}}

He dismisses it with a gesture he does not remember learning.

{{fx:skill_popup:1.3:1.1}}

**Passive Unlocked: {{fx:neon:1.2}}Threshold Sense{{/fx}}**

Grants awareness of structural instability within 4 metres. Triggered by proximity to collapse-risk terrain.

{{/fx}}

The {{fx:cadence:1.1}}cold wall is real. The light is real. The fear is real. That is three things he can verify.{{/fx}} He decides it is enough to stand on.

When he touches the stone and the world does not dissolve, he exhales once and begins.
